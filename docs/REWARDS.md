# Reward assumptions and estimation limits

Defaults checked **3 November 2026** against HSBC's [India Live+ product page](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/credit-cards/products/live-plus/) and [service guide](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/credit-cards/live-plus-credit-card-services-guide.pdf). Check your own current terms and statement; the app is not connected to HSBC.

- 10% on eligible dining/food delivery, groceries, shopping and utilities, capped at a shared ₹1,200 monthly cashback (₹12,000 qualifying spend).
- Most other eligible spending earns 1.5%. Accelerated spending beyond the cap gets no additional cashback in this estimate.
- Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra shopping earn the 1.5% base rate. The [Myntra launch offer](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/offers/myntra-launch-offer-liveplus-tncs.pdf) ended **31 October 2026**. Its optional setting applies only to historical purchases dated **31 July–31 October 2026**, shares the cap and is off by default; transactions after that period return to 1.5%.
- Fuel, international spending and other excluded MCCs should be marked **Excluded**. The app has no complete automatic MCC lookup; refer to the guide's printed pages 22–24 for classifications.

Notification text normally lacks the bank's merchant category code. Merchant-name suggestions are provisional and count in estimates. Unknown categories and possible duplicates are excluded until reviewed. Remembered categories apply to an exact normalized merchant name for new alerts and pasted purchases; revisit them if classification changes. See the [merchant catalog and custom mappings](MERCHANTS.md).

Choose the bank's merchant category, not the particular item purchased. The bank's actual cashback remains authoritative.

Recognized transaction dates are used where available, otherwise notification receipt dates. Bank posting near period boundaries can differ; edit ledger dates when reconciling.

Calendar-month and statement-closing-day modes are available. The initial calendar-month setting is provisional until saved. Changing the period or cap affects all displayed history; historical rule versions are not stored.

Refunds remain excluded until linked to an original purchase. Full/partial refunds reduce that purchase's spend in its original period. The bank may recover cashback in a later statement; reconcile timing separately.

Amounts use integer paise. Cashback is an aggregate estimate and can differ from bank rounding. Welcome offers, annual fee waivers, statement reconciliation and automatic historical-SMS import are outside version 1.
