# Reward assumptions and estimation limits

Defaults checked **31 October 2026** against HSBC's [India Live+ product page](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/credit-cards/products/live-plus/) and [service guide](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/credit-cards/live-plus-credit-card-services-guide.pdf). Check your own current terms and statement; the app is not connected to HSBC.

- 10% on eligible dining/food delivery, groceries, shopping and utilities, capped at a shared ₹1,200 monthly cashback (₹12,000 qualifying spend).
- Most other eligible spending earns 1.5%. Accelerated spending beyond the cap gets no additional cashback in this estimate.
- Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra shopping normally use the base rate. The optional Myntra promotion is limited to **31 July–31 October 2026**, shares the cap and is off by default.
- Fuel, international spending and other excluded MCCs should be marked **Excluded**. The app has no complete automatic MCC lookup; refer to the guide.

Notification text normally lacks the bank's merchant category code. Merchant-name suggestions are provisional and count in estimates. Unknown categories and possible duplicates are excluded until reviewed. Remembered categories apply to an exact normalized merchant name; revisit them if classification changes.

Choose the bank's merchant category, not the particular item purchased. The bank's actual cashback remains authoritative.

Recognized transaction dates are used where available, otherwise notification receipt dates. Bank posting near period boundaries can differ; edit ledger dates when reconciling.

Calendar-month and statement-closing-day modes are available. The initial calendar-month setting is provisional until saved. Changing the period or cap affects all displayed history; historical rule versions are not stored.

Refunds remain excluded until linked to an original purchase. Full/partial refunds reduce that purchase's spend in its original period. The bank may recover cashback in a later statement; reconcile timing separately.

Amounts use integer paise. Cashback is an aggregate estimate and can differ from bank rounding. Welcome offers, annual fee waivers, statement reconciliation and automatic historical-SMS import are outside version 1.
