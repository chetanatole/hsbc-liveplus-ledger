# Device testing checklist

1. Install on Android 8+ and open Settings. Confirm the screen scrolls with the keyboard open, text remains readable, and controls are reachable.
2. Enter card suffix, period and cap and confirm valid edits apply automatically. In Notifications, select one SMS messaging app, enable capture and grant notification access. In SMS, enable capture, read the disclosure and grant incoming-SMS permission. Return to Overview and check **Capture enabled**. Test each edition separately.
3. Paste a **redacted copy** of an existing purchase alert using your configured suffix. Preview the parsed amount, merchant and date before adding. Correct the category and confirm the cap meter updates. Delete the test row if it duplicates a real purchase.
4. Wait for the next ordinary HSBC card purchase alert. Check that exactly one purchase appears, or that an extra source alert is flagged as a possible duplicate. Confirm the amount and date against the SMS. Do not make a purchase merely to test the app.
5. Check that an OTP, failed purchase and card bill payment are not listed as purchases. If a format is not recognized, manually add the purchase. A sanitized alert example can be used to improve the parser later.
6. Check the previous/next period boundaries against your statement, especially a transaction on the closing day. Adjust the ledger date when bank posting moves it to the next statement.
7. For a refund, link it to the correct purchase. Check that the original period's eligible spend reduces and compare the actual bank clawback separately.
8. Export a backup and confirm a JSON file is created. Test restore on a spare device or only after securing the backup; restore requires an empty ledger and keeps capture off.
9. Revoke capture access (notification access or SMS permission) and confirm Overview reports capture off. Re-enable it if you want capture to continue. Open the app after restarting the phone to confirm its status.
10. In a period containing suggested, uncategorized, possible-duplicate and unlinked-refund transactions, check the highlighted **Needs review** count. Toggle the filter, resolve items and confirm the count decreases. Confirm linked refunds, checked purchases, deleted rows and other periods do not inflate the count.
11. Scroll a long Activity list. Confirm **Add purchase**, **Paste alert** and **Needs review** stay visible. Switch to another app and return, switch tabs, and rotate the screen; check the selected period, review filter and scroll position are retained appropriately.
12. Confirm transaction details show only **Alert**, **SMS**, **Pasted** or **Manual** as the source, including transactions captured by earlier versions. Confirm the header has no private/offline badge and Settings has no Android version requirement.
13. Confirm **Settings** opens with card details and automatic capture first, followed by **Appearance**. In **Appearance**, confirm the only choices are **Light** and **Dark**, Light is used without a saved choice, and the choice survives reopening the app. Check Overview, Activity, review/empty states, status/navigation bars, purchase/refund/paste dialogs, category/source menus, switches, checkboxes and input hints for readable colors. Confirm unselected tabs and the review filter have visible backgrounds and outlines in dark mode, selections are distinct, and taps/focus show feedback. Switch themes after scrolling and check the scroll position is retained.
14. Save a purchase with **Remember this merchant’s category** checked, then reopen it and another purchase with the same merchant/category; confirm the checkbox is checked. Change the category while checked and save; confirm the remembered rule updates. Uncheck and save; confirm reopening shows unchecked and other existing transactions are unchanged. Cancel an edit after toggling the checkbox and confirm the saved rule is unchanged. Saving an older purchase with a different category without touching its unchecked box must preserve the existing merchant rule. Confirm **Needs category** cannot be remembered.
15. Confirm the Overview cashback card omits the bank-posting/rounding sentence and the other eligible spend line displays the 1.5% cashback rate.
16. Confirm Overview and Settings remain scrollable without scrollbars; Activity keeps its scrollbar. Check Appearance has no description, Add purchase uses the same label on Overview and Activity, and the empty-period description has no setup requirement. In purchase editing, open the calendar on an existing date, choose a date across a month/year boundary and save; reopen to verify the date. Cancel the calendar and confirm the existing date stays unchanged. Test the picker in Light and Dark. Confirm the four accelerated categories show 10%, Other eligible shows 1.5%, and Excluded shows 0%.
17. Confirm Appearance displays only **Light** or **Dark**, the rewards checked date is **3 Nov 2026**, and the Myntra option is clearly historical. For Myntra suggestions, verify the optional 10% offer stops after 31 October 2026 and later transactions receive the 1.5% base category.
18. In **Settings → Merchant categories → Manage merchant categories**, add an exact-name mapping, edit its category, rename it and delete it. Cancel each operation once and confirm no change. Confirm case/space variants match, existing mappings cannot be accidentally overwritten by a rename, invalid/unknown categories are rejected, and no existing transactions change. Confirm only remembered/user-added mappings appear, with Add mapping and Close controls and no built-in list, in both themes. Preview a pasted purchase for a saved merchant and confirm it uses the saved category; captured purchases should use the same rule. Confirm Swiggy Instamart and Blink Commerce suggest Groceries, Infiniti Retail suggests Shopping, and Airtel Payments Bank or a gateway name alone stays uncategorized.
19. In **Overview → This period**, check that the 10% and 1.5% groups show spend and estimated cashback, including zero amounts in an empty period. Confirm the excluded 0% group shows only spend, with no cashback sub-row. Confirm the cashback components sum to the total, the 10% amount agrees with the accelerated card and respects the configured cap, and linked refunds reduce the appropriate component. Check layout and readability in Light and Dark with large amounts and larger font sizes.
20. With notification access and capture enabled, leave Live+ Ledger in the background and check the next ordinary card alert without making a purchase just for testing. Repeat after screen-off/idle time. If capture only works with the app open, check battery/background restrictions and any sleeping-app list, then test again. Keep transaction notifications enabled in the selected source app and confirm the captured amount against the alert.
21. Confirm the total block matches the category layout: **Total spend** in the larger font, **Total estimated cashback** beneath it in the smaller font. Check that total spend includes uncategorized and excluded purchases after linked refunds, while possible duplicates are excluded until confirmed. Check an empty period and large amounts in both themes.
22. In Samsung Messages, send a synthetic purchase, OTP-like test message and another purchase without clearing notifications. Test OTP first, multiple purchases, identical purchases, screen-off/background use and a fresh alert after clearing the group. The notification version can miss content Android hides. Readable grouped history must not replay older purchases. Use Paste alert after checking for an existing entry. The SMS version should capture a separately delivered ordinary purchase without clearing notifications. Do not use real OTPs or make an unnecessary purchase.
23. Verify there is no Capture diagnostics or Copy report control in either version. On update, any previous capture_diagnostics preference should be removed. Transaction replay fingerprints must still prevent duplicate history imports. Both launcher/app-info names, header and notification-access service label must read Live+ Ledger without SMS/Notifications/edition suffixes. The app should contain no edition comparisons or recommendations to install another version. User-facing application copy must contain no semicolons.

## Two-edition acceptance checks

24. On Notifications, verify the picker lists only installed SMS-capable apps and preselects the default when available. A radio selection applies immediately and closes the picker. Close without selecting must leave the value unchanged. Check legacy multi-source migration, an uninstalled source, a changed default and no available SMS app. No bank/unrelated apps or SMS permission request should appear. Overview and Settings must show the same highlighted explanation that Android may hide alerts grouped with an OTP and that the conversation notification should be cleared before the next alert. The SMS version must not show this note. Changing source while capture is on must not import already-visible history.
25. On SMS, verify there is no source-app picker, notification-access flow, permanent installer-warning paragraph or edition label. Turn on capture with valid card inputs. Read the new-incoming-SMS-only disclosure, cancel once and deny the Android permission once. Capture must remain off. Retry explicitly and grant access. Permission-denial guidance should appear only on failure, without automatic prompt loops or references to another version. A successful capture-request grant starts capture. A permission-only grant leaves capture off until switched on.
26. With a synthetic purchase SMS already in the inbox, install/open/enable the SMS edition. Confirm that old message is not imported. Send new synthetic OTP, unrelated, failed and other-card SMS: no purchases should be saved. Send a matching purchase after the OTP without clearing notifications; when Android delivers the SMS, exactly one purchase should be recorded with source SMS. Test multipart messages and two identical purchases sent at different times. Confirm repeated delivery does not add the same event again. RCS/MMS must not be represented as supported SMS capture; no real OTP or unnecessary purchase is needed.
27. In SMS, check background/screen-off receipt, capture off/on, permission revoke/regrant and process recreation. Open the app after installation or force-stop. Confirm manual entry and Paste alert work without SMS permission. Check the concise capture description and last captured purchase timestamp.
28. Export a backup, then install the same-release SMS APK over Notifications without uninstalling. Ledger/card settings/theme/merchant mappings must remain while capture is off and prior fingerprint caches are cleared. Enable the new access method, then switch back and repeat. Both use Live+ Ledger as the name and the same version/signing identity. Verify same-release switching and normal same-edition updates. Do not erase data to force an incompatible downgrade or signing change.
29. Run `tools/build-all.ps1`. Both artifacts must pass `tools/verify-apk.ps1`: Notifications has no uses-permissions or SMS receiver; SMS has only `RECEIVE_SMS`, a receiver protected by `BROADCAST_SMS`, and no notification listener or source-app queries. Neither has `READ_SMS`, `SEND_SMS`, `INTERNET` or an inbox URI. Check both lines in `SHA256SUMS.txt` against the APKs, and review README/installation/edition/privacy/release instructions for consistent filenames and new-message-only claims.
30. Check automatic settings writes with no Save settings button. Edit suffix, day and cap, pause, navigate away, background the app before the debounce expires and recreate/reopen. Valid values must persist and update Overview. Empty/partial/invalid inputs must show inline errors and leave prior values unchanged. A different suffix must be rejected when transactions exist. A new manual-only setup using default day/cap should complete after entering a valid suffix. Promotion, theme and source changes should apply immediately. Typing must not move focus or reset scroll, and must never request a permission. Transaction/mapping edits still require their own Save.
31. Toggle capture off while field edits are pending. It must stop immediately. Toggle on with invalid card fields, missing access or no valid messaging source. Capture must remain off until values/access are valid. Test notification-permission approval/cancel/denial, rotation while requesting permission and returning through App settings. Only an explicit pending enable action should start capture after approval.
